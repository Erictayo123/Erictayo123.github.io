// 01  about

About

I'm currently studying Electrical Engineering, with a growing focus on embedded systems — the layer where code meets hardware. Most of my work lives somewhere between a microcontroller and a machine learning model: getting a neural network small enough, and a circuit reliable enough, that the two can actually work together as a symphony.

My interests span Coding, TinyML, computer architecture, RISC-V, FPGAs, and digital design. I don't come from a background where any of this was easy — I was weak in math and physics early on, and most of what I know now came from building things until they worked, then figuring out why. That's how I learn things.

Outside of coursework, I help maintain a small community WiFi network, tinker with reverse engineering and CTF challenges, and try to keep a disciplined routine — early mornings, consistent training, steady progress. Long term, I'm aiming toward graduate study and a career built around hardware that thinks for itself.
